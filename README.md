# Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis

## Project Overview

This project presents a **Microsoft Power BI dashboard** developed using the Superstore dataset. The analysis evaluates sales performance, profitability, customer segments, products, shipping methods, and regional performance.

The goal is to transform transactional sales data into meaningful business insights that can support management decision-making.

## Business Objective

The analysis aims to:

- Evaluate overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify high- and low-performing regions.
- Analyse sales performance across customer segments and product categories.
- Identify the most profitable products and sub-categories.
- Examine sales trends over time.
- Understand shipping-mode usage.
- Provide actionable recommendations for improving business performance.

## Tools Used

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Data modelling, DAX measures and dashboard development
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **Microsoft Excel** – Dataset/source data

## Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes:

- Sales & profit by region
- Sales trend over time
- Top 5 most profitable products
- Sales distribution by state
- Profit by sub-category
- Sales by customer segment
- Orders by ship mode
- Category performance summary
- Interactive slicers for Region, Category and Segment
- Executive key-insights section

## Key Insights

1. **West region leads overall sales performance**, generating approximately **$0.73M** in sales.
2. **Consumer customers contribute the largest share of sales**, generating approximately **$1.16M**.
3. **Technology is the strongest-performing category**, generating approximately **$836K in sales** and **$145K in profit**.
4. **Copiers are the most profitable sub-category**, generating approximately **$56K in profit**.
5. **Standard Class is the most frequently
6. used shipping mode**, accounting for approximately **59.77% of orders**.
